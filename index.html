Feasibility Period

Over 350 people were engaged with during the first phase of the review. This helped the council to build a clearer picture of the challenges people face when parking in and around the town centre and railway station. Alongside technical studies measuring parking pressure at different days, this fed into a wider study of the area.

Detailed designs have been prepared that best represents the community’s demands based on the feedback. A statutory consultation is currently underway to confirm the final designs and make any modifications or mitigations in response to feedback from the community.
